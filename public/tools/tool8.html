ΔP Finder

Step 1 of 5 — The concept
Sizing a valve or actuator needs ΔP — the pressure dropped across it. ΔP isn't guessed. It falls out once you know P2.
ΔP = P1 − P2
  • P1 — your supply or pump pressure. Usually already known.
  • P2 — the pressure your device needs downstream to actually do its job. This is the number people get stuck on.
  • Find P2 first. ΔP is just what's left over.
Step 2 of 5 — What are you sizing?
A Convert (cylinders, rack-&-pinion, vane actuators)
Device converts pressure into force/torque via known geometry.
B Accumulate (ball/control valves, glycol or water loops)
No conversion factor — P2 comes from what's downstream.
